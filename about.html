ABOUT

Vanity Blue is an independent music platform based in Durham.

Founded by James Fish and Jack Whyte, Vanity Blue was created from a shared love of music and a belief that discovering something new should still feel exciting and personal.

Through resident shows, guest mixes, live broadcasts, events and editorial projects, Vanity Blue exists as a space for artists, selectors and listeners to discover music outside the usual channels.
